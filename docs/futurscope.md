# 🌾 Trusted Farm-to-Market Digital Marketplace with Price Intelligence

## 📌 Problem Statement Alignment

Farmers often receive unfair prices due to:
- Lack of market transparency
- Dependence on intermediaries
- No demand visibility
- Limited trust mechanisms
- Post-harvest inefficiencies

This project aims to build a **digital farm-to-buyer marketplace** enhanced with **AI-driven price intelligence, demand forecasting, and trust scoring mechanisms**.

---

# 🚀 Core Features

## 1️⃣ Direct Farmer-to-Buyer Connectivity

- Farmer product listing (crop name, quantity, expected price, location)
- Buyer marketplace dashboard
- Direct chat & negotiation system
- Order placement & tracking
- Multi-category crop support (grains, vegetables, fruits, etc.)
- Geo-based buyer recommendations

✅ Matches Requirement: Direct farmer-to-buyer connectivity

---

## 2️⃣ Real-Time Market Price Intelligence

- Live mandi price integration (via government/open datasets)
- Regional price comparison
- Historical price trend visualization
- Price fluctuation graphs
- Smart selling recommendation:
  - "Best time to sell"
  - "Hold or Sell suggestion"

AI Component:
- Time-series price prediction (ARIMA / Prophet / LSTM)

✅ Matches Requirement: Real-time price intelligence & price comparison tools

---

## 3️⃣ Demand Forecasting System

- Seasonal demand prediction
- Crop demand analytics by region
- Future price forecasting
- Market supply-demand heatmaps

AI Component:
- LSTM-based forecasting
- Seasonal decomposition
- Trend analysis

Output:
- Predicted demand for next 7/15/30 days
- Expected price change percentage

✅ Matches Requirement: Demand forecasting

---

## 4️⃣ Trust & Reputation Mechanism

- Buyer trust score (0–100 scale)
- Farmer reliability score
- Rating & review system
- Delivery timeliness scoring
- Payment reliability tracking
- Fraud/anomaly detection alerts

AI Component:
- Classification model for fraud detection
- Behavior anomaly detection

✅ Matches Requirement: Trust and reputation mechanisms

---

## 5️⃣ Smart Logistics & Inefficiency Reduction

- Nearest buyer suggestion
- Transportation distance optimization
- Bulk order matching
- Supply clustering by region
- Harvest-to-sale time tracking

Optional:
- Route optimization algorithm

✅ Matches Requirement: Reduced post-harvest and logistical inefficiencies
